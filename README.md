# Sung-hyun Yoon

**Computer Engineering undergraduate · Hoseo University**

I’m interested in embedded systems, sensor data pipelines, and applied machine learning. My work includes a smart care mat prototype and research on how software turns measurements into useful alerts and control decisions.

## Projects

### HERO Smart Care Mat · iTurtle Lab
**2026 · Team project**

A pressure-sensing mat prototype designed to help caregivers notice sustained pressure and review alerts in a mobile app. My role focused on the pressure-sensor matrix, microcontroller data acquisition, and firmware. The team’s system connects an ESP32-based sensing device to the app through MQTT and a backend service.

### Codyssey Software Immersion · Team Spacebar
**2024 · Team software project**

Worked with a five-person team in Hoseo University’s three-week, peer-learning software program. Team Spacebar received recognition at both the campus showcase and the 2024 INNO-CON. [Program overview](https://innovationacademy.kr/academy/board/read?boardManagementNo=2&boardNo=285&level=2&menuNo=16)

**KIICE Fall Conference · Oct 2026**  
Sung-hyun Yoon, Su-jin Lee, and Jin-suk Bang

### A Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning-Based Models for Tax Audit Target Selection and Proposals for Improvement
**KIICE Spring Conference · May 2026 · Poster and proceedings, pp. 605–608**  
Sung-hyun Yoon, Su-jin Lee, and Jin-suk Bang
